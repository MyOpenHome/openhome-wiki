### definition
Voice-first refers to design philosophies and technologies that prioritize voice interactions as the primary user interface. This concept emphasizes the use of natural language processing and speech recognition to enable users to engage with devices and services primarily through spoken commands rather than traditional input methods such as keyboards or touch screens.

### why-it-matters
Voice-first is significant to OpenHome as it aligns with the platform’s vision of creating intuitive and seamless user experiences. By focusing on voice-based interactions, OpenHome can cater to a wider audience, including those with disabilities or those who prefer hands-free operation. This approach enhances accessibility and convenience, making it easier for users to interact with various features and functionalities of the platform.

### how-it-manifests
In the OpenHome platform, voice-first technology manifests through features such as voice-controlled navigation, the ability to set reminders or alarms using voice commands, and integration with virtual assistants. These functionalities allow users to control their environment or access information without needing to engage with a screen directly.
