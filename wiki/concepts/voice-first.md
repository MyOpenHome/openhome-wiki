### definition
Voice-first is a design approach that prioritizes voice interactions as the primary means of communication between users and technology. It emphasizes the use of speech recognition and natural language processing to enable users to engage with digital devices and services through voice commands.

### why-it-matters
Voice-first technology is significant to OpenHome as it simplifies user interactions, making technology more accessible and user-friendly. With voice as a primary interface, users can engage with various services without needing to rely on traditional input methods like keyboards or touchscreens. This approach caters to diverse user needs and enhances user experience.

### how-it-manifests
In the OpenHome platform, voice-first capabilities can be seen in features such as voice-activated commands for controlling smart home devices, interacting with applications, and retrieving information. The integration of voice recognition technology allows users to issue commands, ask questions, and receive responses in a conversational manner, promoting a more intuitive and seamless user experience.
