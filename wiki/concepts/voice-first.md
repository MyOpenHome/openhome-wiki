## definition
Voice-first refers to a computing approach that prioritizes voice interaction as the primary user interface, allowing users to engage with technology using spoken commands rather than traditional input methods like keyboards or touch screens.

## why-it-matters
Voice-first technology is increasingly relevant to OpenHome as it enhances accessibility and fosters a more natural user experience. By leveraging voice interaction, OpenHome can ensure that its platform is user-friendly and accommodating for a wider audience, including those who may have difficulties using conventional interfaces.

## how-it-manifests
This concept is expected to manifest in OpenHome through features that allow users to control applications, access information, and execute tasks purely through voice commands. Integrating voice-first capabilities can improve user engagement and streamline interactions within the platform.
