### Definition
Voice-first refers to a design philosophy and interaction model where voice commands and spoken language are the primary means of interaction with technology, superseding visual interfaces such as screens.

### Why It Matters
Voice-first technology is significant for OpenHome as it enhances accessibility and user engagement. By prioritizing voice interactions, OpenHome caters to users who may find traditional interfaces challenging, thereby broadening its user base and enabling a more intuitive communication method with digital services.

### How It Manifests
In the OpenHome platform, voice-first features can be seen in how users initiate commands, control devices, and access information through speech. Integration of voice recognition systems and natural language processing allows for seamless interaction, creating a more immersive and user-friendly experience.
