## definition
Voice-First refers to the design philosophy and approach where voice interactions are prioritized as the primary mode of communication with technology, rather than traditional text or graphical interfaces. This concept embraces the use of voice recognition and natural language processing to enhance user experiences.

## why-it-matters
Voice-First is significant to OpenHome as it transforms how users engage with the platform. By facilitating interactions through voice, it aims to create more intuitive and accessible user experiences, enabling users to accomplish tasks hands-free and seamlessly integrate technology into their everyday lives.

## how-it-manifests
In OpenHome, the Voice-First concept can manifest through voice-activated commands, enabling users to control devices, access information, or perform tasks simply by speaking. It enhances user accessibility and convenience, removing barriers that text-based inputs may impose.
