### definition
Voice-first refers to the design and implementation of user interfaces primarily controlled through voice commands, rather than traditional input methods such as keyboards or touchscreens. This concept emphasizes the use of spoken language for interactions with digital devices and applications.

### why-it-matters
Voice-first technology is crucial for OpenHome as it enhances accessibility and usability, allowing users to interact with the platform in a more natural and intuitive way. As voice technology continues to evolve, it opens up new avenues for user engagement and expands the user base by catering to individuals who may have difficulty using traditional interfaces.

### how-it-manifests
In OpenHome, the voice-first approach is integrated into various applications and services, enabling users to perform tasks, access information, and control smart devices through voice commands. This functionality creates a seamless experience, facilitating hands-free operation and making interactions simpler and more efficient.
