Voice-first refers to designing interactions primarily through voice commands, allowing users to engage with technology using natural language rather than traditional input methods like keyboards or touchscreens.

Voice-first interactions are essential in the context of OpenHome as they enhance user accessibility and convenience. This approach enables users to control their home environments seamlessly and naturally, making technology more intuitive and integrated into daily life.
