### definition
Voice-first refers to a design and interaction approach where voice commands serve as the primary method for users to engage with technology. This paradigm emphasizes the importance of natural language processing and voice user interfaces over traditional visual screens.

### why-it-matters
Voice-first is increasingly relevant to OpenHome as it enables more intuitive interactions with devices and applications. By prioritizing voice inputs, OpenHome can enhance user accessibility and convenience, allowing users to manage their smart home technologies more effortlessly.

### how-it-manifests
In the OpenHome platform, voice-first capabilities may be integrated into various applications and devices, offering users the ability to control their environments, access information, and interact with services using voice commands. This can include smart home assistants that respond to vocal inquiries or commands, thereby streamlining the user experience.
