### definition
Voice-first refers to a design approach that prioritizes voice interactions as the primary mode of communication with technology, emphasizing the use of voice recognition and natural language processing.

### why-it-matters
The voice-first paradigm is significant for OpenHome as it enhances user accessibility and offers a more intuitive way to interact with applications and devices. By focusing on voice, OpenHome can cater to diverse user needs and preferences, creating a more inclusive experience.

### how-it-manifests
Voice-first capabilities may appear across various features within the OpenHome platform, including voice commands for controlling smart home devices, interactive voice assistants for providing information, and engaging in conversations that feel natural to users.
