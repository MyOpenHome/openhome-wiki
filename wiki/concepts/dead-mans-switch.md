### definition
A dead man's switch is a safety mechanism that automatically triggers an action or sends a notification if the user becomes incapacitated or fails to respond within a specified time frame. It is designed to ensure continuity and prevent unintended consequences in situations where timely human intervention is critical.

### why-it-matters
In the context of OpenHome, a dead man's switch can enhance user safety and system reliability. By integrating this concept, OpenHome can provide a layer of assurance for users who depend on the automated systems, ensuring that if the system's operator is unable to maintain control, appropriate measures are taken automatically.

### how-it-manifests
While specific implementations within OpenHome are not detailed, the concept of a dead man's switch can be applied in scenarios such as home automation systems, where users can be alerted if they do not interact with their devices for a certain period. This could manifest as automated alerts sent to emergency contacts or system shutdowns to prevent hazards.
