## definition
A dead man's switch is a safety mechanism that is designed to activate a specific action if a user becomes incapacitated or fails to respond. In the context of digital systems, it often serves as a way to ensure that a process continues or triggers a defined protocol if the user does not perform regular actions.

## why-it-matters
In OpenHome, a dead man's switch is crucial for maintaining security and continuity in user interactions. It helps prevent unauthorized access and ensures that users remain in control of their connected environments, particularly in scenarios where user attention or responsiveness may be an issue.

## how-it-manifests
The dead man's switch concept can be implemented in various ways within the OpenHome platform. For instance, it may involve periodic prompts that require user interaction, or automated responses triggered by lack of activity, ensuring that the system behaves predictably even in the absence of direct user action.
