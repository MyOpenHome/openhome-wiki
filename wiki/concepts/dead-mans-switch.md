### definition
A Dead Man's Switch is a safety mechanism that automatically triggers an action when the operator becomes incapacitated or unable to perform a required task. It is typically designed to ensure that certain tasks are completed or that systems are shut down safely if a user does not actively ensure that the task continues.

### why-it-matters
In the context of OpenHome, a Dead Man's Switch can enhance user security and reliability by ensuring that key functions or systems remain under control and are safeguarded against potential failures or user incapacity. This is particularly important in scenarios where automated systems are handling sensitive data or critical functions.
