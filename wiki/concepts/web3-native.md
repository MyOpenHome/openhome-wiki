## definition
Web3 Native refers to applications, services, and platforms that are built on decentralized technologies, particularly those utilizing blockchain. These solutions are characterized by their ability to empower users with control over their data, identity, and assets in a peer-to-peer architecture.

## why-it-matters
Web3 Native technologies are integral to the vision of OpenHome, as they promote user sovereignty, privacy, and security. By incorporating decentralized principles, OpenHome aims to create a more equitable digital ecosystem where users can interact without intermediaries and retain ownership of their digital identities and information.

## how-it-manifests
In the OpenHome platform, the Web3 Native concept manifests through features that allow users to manage their digital identities, transact with cryptocurrencies, and interact with decentralized applications (dApps). This integration facilitates a more transparent and secure user experience, aligning with the principles of decentralization.
