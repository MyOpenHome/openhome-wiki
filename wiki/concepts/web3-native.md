### definition
Web3 Native refers to the principles and technologies that define the decentralized web, which is built on blockchain technology and facilitates peer-to-peer interactions without the need for intermediaries.

### why-it-matters
This concept is crucial to OpenHome as it underpins the platform's commitment to decentralization and user empowerment. By embracing web3 principles, OpenHome can provide users with greater control over their data and online interactions, fostering trust and transparency.

### how-it-manifests
In the OpenHome platform, web3-native elements may appear in features such as decentralized identity solutions, blockchain-based asset management, or smart contracts that enable users to directly manage their interactions and agreements with minimal reliance on traditional centralized systems.
