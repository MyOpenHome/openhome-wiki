## definition
Web3 native refers to applications, platforms, and systems designed to operate within the decentralized web, utilizing blockchain technology and emphasizing user ownership, privacy, and control over data.

## why-it-matters
The concept of being web3 native is crucial for OpenHome as it aligns with the vision of a decentralized future where users have more power over their digital interactions. It embodies the principles of decentralization that are fundamental to the OpenHome ethos, allowing for greater security, transparency, and user empowerment.

## how-it-manifests
Web3 native features may be integrated into various aspects of the OpenHome platform, such as user interfaces that support decentralized identity management, smart contracts for automating agreements, and mechanisms for users to interact with decentralized applications (dApps) directly. These implementations ultimately aim to enhance user engagement and foster a community-centric environment.
