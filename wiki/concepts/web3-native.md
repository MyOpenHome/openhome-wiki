## definition
Web3 Native refers to applications, platforms, and services that are built on decentralized blockchain technologies, focusing on user ownership and control over data and digital assets.

## why-it-matters
In the context of OpenHome, being Web3 Native is crucial as it aligns with the platform's goal of empowering users through self-sovereignty, privacy, and decentralized governance. It promotes a shift from traditional centralized architectures to systems where users have direct control over their information and interactions.

## how-it-manifests
This concept is reflected in OpenHome through features that leverage decentralized networks for data storage, identity verification, and transaction processing. Users can engage with the platform without a central authority, enhancing security and encouraging innovation.
