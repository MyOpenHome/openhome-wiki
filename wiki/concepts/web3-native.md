## definition
Web3-native refers to applications, services, or systems that are built on decentralized networks, utilizing blockchain technology to provide transparency, security, and community governance. These systems often emphasize user ownership and control over data and digital assets.

## why-it-matters
The relevance of web3-native concepts to OpenHome lies in their potential to empower users with unprecedented levels of autonomy and participation in their digital environments. By leveraging decentralized technologies, OpenHome can enhance user trust, facilitate decentralized governance, and promote interoperability with other web3 applications.

## how-it-manifests
In OpenHome, web3-native features may manifest through integration with blockchain protocols, allowing users to interact with smart contracts, manage decentralized identities, and conduct peer-to-peer transactions. This could also involve creating user-friendly interfaces for users to engage with these decentralized functionalities seamlessly.
