## definition
Web3-native refers to applications, platforms, and technologies that are intrinsically built upon decentralized protocols and blockchain technology. They operate in a user-centric environment where data ownership, security, and privacy are prioritized.

## why-it-matters
The relevance of web3-native concepts to OpenHome lies in their potential to reshape interactions within the platform. By leveraging decentralized technologies, OpenHome can provide users with enhanced control over their data, foster trust through transparency, and enable direct peer-to-peer interactions without the need for intermediaries.

## how-it-manifests
In the OpenHome ecosystem, web3-native features might include decentralized identity systems, user-governed communities, or smart contracts that automate transactions and processes. These elements encourage user participation and engagement while ensuring that the platform evolves in a manner that reflects community needs and preferences.
