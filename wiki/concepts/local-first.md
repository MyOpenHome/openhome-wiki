### definition
Local-First refers to a design approach emphasizing that applications should operate primarily on a user's device rather than relying exclusively on remote servers. This concept aims to reduce latency, ensure accessibility during network outages, and enhance user agency over their data.

### why-it-matters
In the context of OpenHome, Local-First is significant as it aligns with the platform's goal of promoting user empowerment and control over personal data. By facilitating a more resilient and responsive experience, it addresses concerns around privacy and dependence on continuous internet connectivity.

### how-it-manifests
The Local-First concept manifests within OpenHome through features that prioritize local storage and processing of data. Users can interact with applications offline, with changes syncing to the cloud when the connection is restored. This architecture fosters a seamless experience while maintaining user control.
