### definition
Abilities-as-apps refers to the concept of encapsulating various capabilities or functionalities within modular applications that can be easily integrated and utilized across different platforms or devices.

### why-it-matters
This concept is significant to OpenHome because it promotes flexibility and interoperability among different digital tools and services. By allowing users to access diverse abilities in a modular format, OpenHome enhances user experience and fosters a broader ecosystem of resources and applications that can work seamlessly together.

### how-it-manifests
In the OpenHome platform, abilities-as-apps can be observed in the way that various services and features are offered as standalone applications that users can choose to integrate based on their needs. This modular approach allows for easy updates, customization, and scalability of functionalities without disrupting the overall system.
