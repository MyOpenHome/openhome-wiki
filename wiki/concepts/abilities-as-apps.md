### definition
Abilities-as-apps refer to the concept where individual functionalities or features within a platform are treated as standalone applications, allowing for modular use and integration.

### why-it-matters
This concept is significant to OpenHome because it encourages flexibility and customization for users. By allowing various abilities to function as separate applications, users can tailor their experiences according to their specific preferences and needs.

### how-it-manifests
In the OpenHome platform, abilities-as-apps can be seen where functionalities such as communication, task management, and automation can operate independently or be combined based on user requirements. This modular approach enhances user engagement and satisfaction by offering diverse choices.
