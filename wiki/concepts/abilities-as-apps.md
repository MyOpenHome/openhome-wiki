### definition
Abilities-as-apps refers to the concept of modular, self-contained functionalities within the OpenHome platform, allowing users to access various features as individual applications.

### why-it-matters
This concept is relevant to OpenHome because it promotes flexibility and scalability, enabling users to customize their experience by selecting and integrating only the abilities they need. This modular approach enhances user engagement and satisfaction.

### how-it-manifests
Abilities-as-apps can be seen throughout the OpenHome ecosystem where different functionalities or features (like smart home controls, personalization settings, etc.) are available as separate modules that can be easily accessed and utilized by the user.
