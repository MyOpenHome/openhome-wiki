### what-it-is
The Local Link is a component of the OpenHome platform that enables seamless communication and interaction between local devices within a home network.

### how-it-works
Local Link is designed to facilitate the connection of various smart home devices, allowing them to share data and interoperability. It supports protocols that ensure devices can discover and communicate with one another directly, without requiring an external internet connection. This enhances the reliability and speed of device interactions.
