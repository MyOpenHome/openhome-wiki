### what-it-is
The OpenHome marketplace is a digital platform designed for the distribution and discovery of applications, services, and content that enhance the OpenHome experience.

### how-it-works
The marketplace functions as a centralized hub where users can browse, purchase, and download various offerings compatible with OpenHome-enabled devices. It is integrated with the OpenHome ecosystem, allowing seamless interaction between the marketplace and the user's devices. Content can range from third-party applications to unique services, which are enabled through APIs and SDKs provided by OpenHome.
