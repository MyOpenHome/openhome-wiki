### what-it-is
The platform marketplace serves as a digital storefront where users can browse, buy, and sell various applications, tools, and services that enhance the capabilities of the OpenHome ecosystem.

### how-it-works
The marketplace operates by allowing developers to upload their products, which are then made accessible to users within the OpenHome environment. Users can explore various categories, read descriptions, view ratings, and make purchases directly through the platform. The marketplace is designed to seamlessly integrate with OpenHome components, allowing for easy installation and management of purchased content.
