### what-it-is
The OpenHome marketplace is a digital platform designed to facilitate the exchange of various applications, services, and products that enhance the functionality and usability of OpenHome-compatible devices and software.

### how-it-works
The marketplace operates as a centralized hub where users can browse, purchase, and manage applications or services that integrate with the OpenHome ecosystem. Developers can publish their offerings, allowing for a range of functionalities and enhancements for devices within the OpenHome network. The platform supports user feedback, ratings, and updates, ensuring that users can find reliable and useful products.
